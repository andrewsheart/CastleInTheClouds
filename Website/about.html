Andrew Stenger

Co-founder · St. Louis, Missouri

Andrew is a writer from St. Louis and the author of This Was Made For You, a book that began under the backdrop of the Himalayan mountains. A mechanical engineer, songwriter, former jazz club manager, and MBA candidate, he writes about the beauty of human connection and the quiet strength of awareness.

He hopes to increase the value of our daily living through consideration, or reconsideration, of our daily thoughts, habits, intentions, actions, and inactions, and to build bridges across differences in every medium he works in.
